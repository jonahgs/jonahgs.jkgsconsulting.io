Food & fermentation

Escarpment Laboratories

Tools I’ve built

I build the tools
as well as recommend them.

I work hands-on with large language models, agent workflows and automation tools, including n8n, Supabase, Next.js and the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs. The two projects here are my own, not client work. Both follow the same rule: AI does the first pass, and a person checks the result before anything is saved or sent.

Intake and triage

An agent that finds and sorts opportunities

It pulls in email alerts and web sources, scores each item against set criteria and drafts a response for review. Nothing is sent automatically. Built with n8n, Supabase and the OpenAI API.

Photo to record

A shared kitchen-inventory app

A photo of the fridge becomes an editable list of items. Nothing is saved until a person reviews it. Built with Next.js, Supabase and the Anthropic API.
