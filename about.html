About

Founder-led consulting for businesses that need the work to run more clearly.

JKGS Consulting is led by Jonah Greenbaum-Shinder. The work focuses on operational clarity, workflow design, practical AI support, and stronger follow-through.

Approach

Direct, practical, and built for businesses that need useful change.

The core of the work is helping businesses see what is showing up, where the issue lands, what matters now, and what should carry forward.

The style is commercially minded and implementation-oriented.

In practice, that usually means noticing where work is getting stuck, where too much depends on one person, and where a simple system would remove drag.

Point of view

Useful change beats impressive-looking change.

Founder-led

What I tend to notice quickly
