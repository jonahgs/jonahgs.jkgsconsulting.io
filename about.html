About Jonah Greenbaum-Shinder

A practical partner.
A useful way forward.

I’m Jonah Greenbaum-Shinder, founder of JKGS Consulting in Ottawa. I help owner-led businesses improve operations and develop food products through defined projects and ongoing part-time support.

What I bring

Understand the work.
Help carry the change through.

My background spans manufacturing operations, food and fermentation, department building and practical education. I’m comfortable getting into the detail of how work happens, from purchasing and production handoffs to product trials and the people carrying them out.

I can help investigate an operating problem, take on a defined improvement or work through a food-product question. The work may involve a clearer process, structured trials, better use of existing software, or a small automation or AI tool with human review.

You work directly with me, from understanding the problem to putting an agreed change into use. The work can be a defined project or ongoing part-time support.
