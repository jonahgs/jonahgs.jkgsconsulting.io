About Jonah Greenbaum-Shinder

A practical partner.
A useful way forward.

I’m Jonah Greenbaum-Shinder, founder of JKGS Consulting in Ottawa. I help small and midsize businesses understand where work gets stuck and put practical process and systems improvements into use.

What I bring

Understand the work.
Help carry the change through.

My background spans manufacturing operations, food and fermentation, department building and practical education. I’m comfortable getting into the detail: orders, purchasing, procedures and the people doing the work.

I combine that experience with hands-on building: automations and small AI tools, with a person checking anything that matters. When a clearer process or better use of existing software is the right fix, I'll recommend that instead.

You work directly with me, from understanding the problem to putting an agreed change into use. The work can be a defined project or ongoing part-time support.
