Business workflow automation

Less copying.
Clearer follow-through.

Repeated data entry, manual reminders and reports assembled from several files can take time away from running the business.

I can help connect steps in an existing workflow, improve how information is captured or build a small tool for a defined task. We agree what should happen automatically and which exceptions need a person.

If the process itself needs attention, we start with business process improvement. The work can use existing software, a simple automation or an AI-assisted workflow.

Work can include

  • Moving information from forms into working records
  • Reducing duplicate entry between existing tools
  • Preparing recurring reports and review reminders
  • Drafting or organising information for human review

Testing, access, exception handling and ongoing ownership are agreed as part of the project.

Before building

Check the process.
Then choose the tool.

I start by looking at the task, the available information and the people responsible for the result. A clearer handoff or better use of existing software may be enough.

If AI is appropriate, I define the review step, limits and ongoing owner as part of the implementation scope.

Agree the boundaries

  • Inputs, data permissions and source access
  • What the workflow may and may not do
  • How outputs are checked and corrected
  • Testing and acceptance criteria
  • Training, handover and ongoing ownership

Practical questions

Keep the review visible.

Can you automate a workflow without AI?

Yes. Rule-based automation can move information, trigger reminders or prepare a report using existing tools. A clearer form, spreadsheet or software setup may also be enough. I recommend an approach after understanding the task.

What if the process itself needs fixing?

I start there. Unclear decisions, missing information and poor handoffs can make automation add work. The review checks whether a simpler change should come first.

Who checks the result?

The scope identifies the responsible person, the outputs they review and how errors are corrected. Human review is part of the workflow.

What happens to business information?

Before choosing a tool, I agree with you what information it needs, who may access it and which provider or system would process it. Testing uses suitable sample or approved data.
