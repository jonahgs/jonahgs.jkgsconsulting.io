Applied AI & automation

Practical AI,
where it belongs.

AI can help interpret incoming information, prepare drafts and turn messy source material into reviewable records. I start by checking whether a simpler process or existing tool would do the job. Human review, clear limits and ongoing ownership are part of the design.

Possible uses

A defined task.
A person responsible for review.

These are examples of possible workflows, not delivered client results. The scope depends on the task and the people reviewing it.

01 / Enquiries

Triage and clarification drafts

Organise incoming enquiries, flag missing information and prepare a clarification draft for a person to check and send.

02 / Documents

Capture with source-by-source review

Prepare records from documents, keep each source available and have a person check the captured information.

03 / Notes

Summaries for review

Prepare meeting or operational-note summaries so a person can check the details, decisions and next steps.

Before building

Check the process.
Then choose the tool.

I start by looking at the task, the available information and the people responsible for the result. A clearer handoff or better use of existing software may be enough.

If AI is appropriate, I define the review step, limits and ongoing owner as part of the implementation scope.

Agree the boundaries

  • Inputs, data permissions and source access
  • What the workflow may and may not do
  • How outputs are checked and corrected
  • Testing and acceptance criteria
  • Training, handover and ongoing ownership

Practical questions

Keep the review visible.

What if the process itself needs fixing?

I start there. Unclear decisions, missing information and poor handoffs can make automation add work. The review checks whether a simpler change should come first.

Who checks the result?

The scope identifies the responsible person, the outputs they review and how errors are corrected. Human review is part of the workflow.

What happens to business information?

Before choosing a tool, I agree with you what information it needs, who may access it and which provider or system would process it. Testing uses suitable sample or approved data.
