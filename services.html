A few practical questions

Before we start.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Describe the business and the problem you keep running into. The first conversation helps identify a sensible starting point.

Can a project be done remotely?

Much of the research, process work, reporting, and system development can be done remotely across Canada. Ottawa-based visits can be agreed where seeing the work in person would help.

Does every project involve AI?

AI is one possible tool. The right improvement might be a clearer decision, a better handoff, a simple form, or a change in responsibilities.

How are scope and fees agreed?

We agree the problem, intended outcome, deliverables, working arrangements, and fees before starting. A project can be defined around a specific improvement, or support can continue on a part-time basis.
