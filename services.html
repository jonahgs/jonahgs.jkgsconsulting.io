Services

Practical service lines for businesses that need better systems, stronger workflows, and clearer execution.

The work centers on reducing friction, improving operations, and building better ways for work to move. AI support is part of the system where it helps, not the headline.

01

Operations and Workflow Consulting

For businesses where work is getting stuck, handoffs are inconsistent, or too much depends on memory. The goal is clearer movement, better ownership, and less drag.

Typical work includes

  • Workflow reviews and operating diagnostics
  • Process redesign and simplification
  • SOP creation and knowledge capture
  • Reporting and documentation improvements
  • Clarifying ownership, roles, and handoffs

02

AI Systems and Adoption

For businesses that want AI where it genuinely helps. The focus is on practical support for intake, documentation, review, communication, and follow-through.

Typical work includes

  • AI workflow audits and use-case selection
  • Prompt and process design
  • Tool selection and implementation guidance
  • Training around ChatGPT, Claude, and adjacent tools
  • Lightweight automation and enablement support

03

Strategic Advisory

For owner-led businesses that need a clearer outside view of what is showing up, where the issue lands, and what matters now.

Typical work includes

  • Decision support and prioritization
  • Operating structure and systems guidance
  • Founder-level strategy and execution support
  • Project scoping and pilot design
  • Selective funding support where it fits the work
