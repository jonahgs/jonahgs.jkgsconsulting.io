Privacy Policy — Personal Backups & Home Assistant

Last updated: September 12, 2026.

This policy covers the personal applications operated by the owner of jonahkgs@gmail.com: Surface Server Backup, local and laptop backup services, Home Assistant, and Home Assist Web SDK.

Application information · Terms of use

Data accessed and why

  • Backups: selected server, application, Home Assistant and separately configured laptop files are processed for recovery. These can include databases, configuration, integration credentials and data already stored by those applications.
  • Google Drive: the server backup requests drive.file permission to manage files and folders it creates or is explicitly authorized to access. Contents and metadata are used to upload encrypted snapshots, list and verify them, restore files and clean up old snapshots. Account identity and storage quota may be checked to confirm the destination and available space.
  • Google Calendar: authorized calendar identifiers and event details, including titles, times, descriptions and locations, support schedule displays and the owner's automations. Event details are sent to Google when an authorized event-creation action is requested.
  • Google Nest: authorized device identifiers, names, state, sensor readings and event notifications support home monitoring and automation. Permitted control commands are sent to Google. Camera or doorbell streams, snapshots or clips may be processed where supported, enabled and authorized.
  • Google Assistant SDK: text commands and queries are sent to Google Assistant, and responses support the requested controls, information or broadcasts. Responses may contain personal results when the owner's Google account permissions and settings allow them.
  • Authorization: OAuth client credentials and access/refresh tokens maintain the connections the owner authorizes. The permissions requested by each connection are shown during Google's consent flow.

Storage and protection

Home Assistant configuration, credentials and applicable history, caches, logs or saved media are held on the personal server according to its enabled features. Access is controlled through server and application authentication and file permissions.

Restic encrypts backup contents before writing them to its local or cloud repositories. The recovery password is kept separately from the uploaded repository. OAuth tokens are stored in private server configuration for unattended operation. Home Assistant data and credentials may also be included in encrypted application backups.

Sharing and service providers

The applications exchange data with Google for the Drive, Calendar, Nest and Assistant functions described above. Google Cloud messaging may carry Nest device events to Home Assistant. Configured automations may send selected results or notifications to authorized household devices or other destinations selected by the owner.

These applications do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized machine-learning models. Google user data is used to provide and maintain the requested backup, recovery and home automation functions. Authorized users may access data according to the access the owner grants.

The applications' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google's processing is governed by its applicable service terms and privacy policy.

Retention

The server application cloud backup retains seven daily, four weekly and six monthly snapshots, with cleanup performed weekly. Other local or laptop repositories and separately enabled backup tools follow their own settings. A snapshot qualifying for multiple retention categories is stored once.

Home Assistant history, caches, logs and media follow the owner's configuration and cleanup settings; there is no single retention period for every integration. Recovery copies remain until their retention policy removes them or the owner deletes them. Original Google account data follows the owner's settings with Google.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can disable or remove integrations, stop backup services, revoke application access in Google account connections, and change which devices or data are shared with Home Assistant. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically erase previously stored local data or backup snapshots.

The owner can remove retained copies by deleting the relevant local data or backup snapshots and, where applicable, deleting backup folders and emptying their trash in Google Drive. Original Calendar, Nest and Assistant records are managed separately through Google's product and account controls. Authorized users can contact the operator for help with access or deletion.
