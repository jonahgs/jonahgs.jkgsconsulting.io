Services / Ongoing operations support

Keep improvements
moving.

Part-time operations support for small and midsize businesses in Ottawa and across Canada. Practical help with priorities, reporting and putting improvements into use.

Continuity without a full-time role

Some improvements
need regular attention.

You may know what needs work but struggle to keep it moving alongside the daily demands of the business. Or a first project may uncover several smaller improvements worth carrying through.

I work with you within an agreed scope to review what is happening, choose priorities and support implementation and adoption.

Support can include

  • Priorities and an actively maintained action plan
  • Operating reports and regular reviews
  • Process and system improvements
  • Project scoping and implementation
  • Decision preparation and follow-through
  • Team guidance and usable documentation

How the arrangement works

Clear expectations.
Useful continuity.

We agree the scope, responsibilities, time commitment, fees and review rhythm before starting, then review the arrangement as the business’s needs change.

The work

A shared set of priorities.

Define which outcomes need attention, what I will take on, and who makes the decisions.

The commitment

Time that fits the role.

Agree the part-time commitment, meeting rhythm, response expectations, and how we work between check-ins.

The follow-through

A regular look at what works.

Review progress, whether improvements are being used, and where to direct the next round of effort.

Before we start

Find the right fit.

Can we start with a defined project?

Yes. We can begin with a focused review or an implementation project, then decide whether ongoing support would be useful.

What does fractional mean here?

It means ongoing support on a part-time basis. We agree the scope and time commitment, including whether the work is primarily advisory, hands-on implementation, or a combination.

Can you work with an existing operations lead?

Yes. A defined area of work can complement the people already in the business, with responsibilities and decision authority agreed at the start.

A useful next step

Tell me a little about your business and what is getting in the way. We can work out a useful place to start.
