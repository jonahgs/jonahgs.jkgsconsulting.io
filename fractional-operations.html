Part-time operations consulting for priorities, reporting, process improvements, and implementation.

Continuity without a full-time role

Some improvements
need regular attention.

You may know what needs work but struggle to keep it moving alongside the daily demands of the business. Or a first project may uncover several smaller improvements worth carrying through.

I work with you regularly to review what is happening, choose priorities, coordinate the next steps, and build useful improvements.

Discuss ongoing support

Support can include

  • Priorities and an actively maintained action plan
  • Operating reports and regular reviews
  • Process and system improvements
  • Project scoping and implementation
  • Decision preparation and follow-through
  • Team guidance and usable documentation

How the arrangement works

The work

Define which outcomes need attention, what I will take on, and who makes the decisions.

The follow-through

Before we start

Find the right fit.

Can we start with a defined project?

Yes. A first project can establish the working relationship and resolve a clear problem before either of us commits to ongoing support.

What does fractional mean here?

It means ongoing support on a part-time basis. We agree the scope and time commitment, including whether the work is primarily advisory, hands-on implementation, or a combination.

Can you work with an existing operations lead?

Yes. A defined area of work can complement the people already in the business, with responsibilities and decision authority agreed at the start.
