How I work

From opportunity to practical improvement.

I start by understanding what matters to your business and looking at real examples. Not every promising idea deserves a project, and not every problem needs a new tool. For operations and systems work, I use four practical steps.

  1. 01 / Signal

    See what’s happening.

    Look for recurring problems, missed opportunities, and information that isn’t being put to good use.

  2. 02 / Impact

    Understand what it affects.

    Consider the effect on customers, time, cost, capacity and potential growth.

  3. 03 / Priority

    Choose what matters.

    Decide which opportunities or problems are worth addressing and what can wait.

  4. 04 / System

    Put the change to work.

    Test the simplest useful improvement, establish who owns it, and review the results.
