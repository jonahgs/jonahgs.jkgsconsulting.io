Approach

Diagnose, design, build, and embed.

The method is simple: identify what is breaking down, choose the right level of intervention, build the lightest durable system, and make sure it gets used.

01

Diagnose

Identify what is showing up, where it lands, and what kind of problem it actually is.

02

Design

Choose the smallest meaningful fix, whether that is a decision, template, workflow, or review structure.

03

Build

Put the system in place through forms, handoffs, SOPs, dashboards, and AI-supported workflow assists where useful.

04

Embed

Define ownership, review rhythm, and usage habits so the system holds under real operating pressure.
